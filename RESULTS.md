## Where the wrong answers go

Of 470 graded answerable questions:

- **165 correct**
- **69 answered and wrong**
- **133 refused with a gold session in the evidence** — a calibration failure, the answerer's fault
- **103 refused with the gold session never retrieved** — refusing was correct on what it was shown; the miss is retrieval's

Those last two are separated everywhere in this repo because they need opposite fixes. Merging them into one "false refusal" number points the diagnosis at the wrong layer, which happened here before the split existed.

## Headroom

Converting the 133 calibration failures at the answerer's own precision of 0.7051 would land near **0.551**.

That is an estimate and not a result. It is here because it sizes the gap: over-refusal is worth roughly 20 accuracy points, which is why it is reported as the finding rather than as an excuse.
